# SOP excerpt: Line 3 packaging (synthetic example)

Products are SKU A and SKU B, run in campaigns.

1. Depalletizer - about 9 s per tray, automatic.
2. Filler - about 12 s per tray, buffer of 20 trays in front, changeover 5 min between products, breaks down every 2 h for about 10 min.
3. Capper - about 10 s per tray, buffer of 10, breaks down every 3 h for about 5 min.
4. Labeler - about 10.5 s per tray, buffer of 10, needs an operator.
5. Case packer - about 20 s per case, two parallel packers, 2 at a time, buffer of 12, needs an operator.
